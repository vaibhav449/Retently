# Cell biology

## Organelles

Mitochondria are the site of aerobic respiration. They make most of the cell's ATP, which is why muscle cells, which use a lot of energy, have many of them.

The nucleus holds the cell's DNA and controls which proteins the cell makes. It is surrounded by a double membrane called the nuclear envelope.

Ribosomes build proteins.

## Membranes

- The cell membrane is a phospholipid bilayer.
- It is selectively permeable: small non-polar molecules cross it freely, while ions need channel proteins.
- Cholesterol keeps it fluid at low temperatures.
