# The French Revolution

The French Revolution began in 1789, when financial crisis and food shortages led Louis XVI to call the Estates-General for the first time since 1614.

### Causes

Enlightenment ideas about popular sovereignty and equal rights spread through pamphlets and salons, and gave the Third Estate a language for its grievances against the privileges of the clergy and nobility.

#### The storming of the Bastille

On 14 July 1789 a Paris crowd stormed the Bastille, a royal fortress and prison, to seize gunpowder. The day is now France's national holiday.
